GOD OF WAR RAGNARÖK — CHARACTERS

Explore the Character of the GOD OF WAR RAGNAROK.

THE NINE REALMS — CHARACTERS

KRATOS

THE GOD OF WAR

Kratos is a Spartan warrior and the former Greek God of War. In God of War Ragnarök, he tries to protect his son Atreus while facing the dangers of the Nine Realms.

Abilities

  • Spartan Rage
  • Powerful melee combat
  • Mastery of multiple weapons

Weapon

  • Leviathan Axe
  • Blades of Chaos
  • Draupnir Spear

Atreus

THE LOKI

Kratos's son, also known as Loki. Atreus seeks to understand his identity and discover the truth about the Giants.

Abilities

  • Archery
  • Runic magic
  • Animal transformation

Weapon

Talon Bow

Freya

THE VANIR QUEEN

A powerful Vanir goddess and former Queen of the Valkyries. Freya initially seeks revenge against Kratos but later becomes his ally.

Abilities

  • Vanir magic
  • Archery
  • Seiðr spells

Weapons

Sword and magical bow

Mimir

THE SMARTEST MAN ALIVE

A wise advisor who travels with Kratos. Mimir shares knowledge about Norse mythology, the realms and their inhabitants.

Skills

  • Knowledge of the Nine Realms
  • Storytelling
  • Strategic advice

Role

Advisor and companion

Thor

THE GOD OF THUNDER

The formidable Norse god of thunder and son of Odin. Thor is sent to confront Kratos and Atreus.

Abilities

  • Lightning manipulation
  • Superhuman strength
  • Devastating combat

Weapon

Mjölnir

Odin

THE ALL-FATHER

The ruler of Asgard who seeks knowledge and control over the future. Odin uses manipulation and magic to pursue his goals.

Abilities

  • Seiðr magic
  • Shapeshifting and deception
  • Extensive knowledge

Role

Ruler of Asgard and central antagonist

Týr

THE NORSE GOD OF WAR

The Norse god of war, associated with peace and diplomacy. His fate is closely connected to the conflict surrounding Asgard.

Skills

  • Combat expertise
  • Diplomacy
  • Knowledge of different cultures

Role

God of War

Heimdall

THE WATCHMAN OF ASGARD

Odin's son and guardian of Asgard. Heimdall's extraordinary perception makes him exceptionally difficult to surprise.

Abilities

  • Anticipation of opponents
  • Exceptional reflexes
  • Skilled combat

Role

Guardian of Asgard

Brok

THE DWARVEN BLACKSMITH

A blunt but loyal dwarf who helps Kratos by crafting and upgrading equipment.

Skills

  • Weapon crafting
  • Equipment upgrades
  • Dwarven engineering

Role

Blacksmith and ally

Sindri

THE DWARVEN INVENTOR

Brok's brother and a meticulous craftsman who provides equipment and technical assistance to Kratos and Atreus.

Skills

  • Blacksmithing
  • Inventing
  • Dwarven technology

Role

Craftsman and ally

Angrboda

THE LAST OF THE GIANTS

A young Giant who helps Atreus understand his heritage and the prophecies surrounding the Giants.

Abilities

  • Giant heritage and magic
  • Knowledge of Giant history
  • Combat assistance

Role

Atreus's friend and ally

Surtr

THE FIRE GIANT

An ancient fire Giant whose role becomes crucial to the events of Ragnarök.

Abilities

  • Immense fire power
  • Giant strength
  • Ragnarök-related power

Role

Fire Giant of Muspelheim

Faye

THE PROTECTOR

Kratos's second wife and Atreus's mother. Her final wishes set the journey of Kratos and Atreus in motion.

Skills

  • Leviathan Axe wielder
  • Giant heritage
  • Skilled warrior

Role

Kratos's wife and Atreus's mother

Baldur

THE AESIR GOD

Odin's son and the primary antagonist of the 2018 God of War. A spell prevents him from feeling physical sensations, shaping his tragic story.

Abilities

  • Powerful hand-to-hand combat
  • Rapid healing
  • Superhuman strength

Role

Antagonist in God of War (2018)
